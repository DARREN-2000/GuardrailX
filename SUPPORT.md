# Support

We're here to help you succeed with GuardrailX! Here are the best ways to get support.

## Community Support

For general questions, discussions, and help from the community, please use:

*   **GitHub Discussions:** [Join the conversation](https://github.com/DARREN-2000/GuardrailX/discussions)
*   **GitHub Issues:** For bug reports and feature requests, [open an issue](https://github.com/DARREN-2000/GuardrailX/issues).

## Enterprise Support

GuardrailX is designed for enterprise-grade workloads. If you require dedicated support, custom integrations, SLAs, or deployment assistance, please contact us at **sales@guardrailx.com**.
